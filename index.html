PhD Candidate · Systems Neuroscience · FMI Basel

How does a neural circuit learn to make sense of the world? My research tackles this question in the olfactory system, where the mapping from stimulus to percept is anything but fixed. Using zebrafish as a genetically tractable vertebrate model, I combine whole-brain imaging, electrophysiology, and behavioral assays to understand how piriform-homolog circuits reorganize their representations through experience — and what this tells us about cortical computation more broadly.
